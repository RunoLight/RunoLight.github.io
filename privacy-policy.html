Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in Android, IOS mobile games developed by Vasily Kuznetsov / Red Pixel Games.

1. Information We Collect

We do not operate our own backend servers and do not directly store personal information provided by players on our servers.

The game may use third-party services that automatically collect certain technical and usage information, such as device information, app events, diagnostics, approximate location, advertising identifiers, and other information required for analytics or service operation.

2. Third-Party Services

Depending on the version of the game, we may use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase - analytics, crash reporting and other app services.
  • AppMetrica - application analytics and usage statistics.
  • Other third-party services required for analytics, advertising, authentication or technical operation of the game.

These services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase Privacy Information
  • Yandex Metrica Privacy Policy

3. Analytics

We use analytics services to understand how players use the game, measure performance, detect problems, and improve gameplay and user experience.

Analytics data is generally collected automatically by the corresponding third-party services. We do not use analytics to directly identify individual players.

4. Data Retention

We do not maintain our own database of player personal information. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their respective policies and settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
